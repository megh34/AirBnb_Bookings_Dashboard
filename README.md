# 🏡 Airbnb Bookings Analysis (2019)

This project explores Airbnb booking data from 2019 to uncover key trends, neighborhood profitability, pricing structures, and customer behavior insights.

## 🔍 Project Overview

The analysis is divided into the following key focus areas:

- **📌 Price Category & Room Type**: Comparison of listing volume by price tier and accommodation type.
- **🗺️ Highest Earning Neighborhoods**: Visual mapping of top revenue-generating areas in NYC.
- **💰 Revenue vs Neighborhood Group**: Revenue contribution share across boroughs.
- **🏘️ Room Distribution**: Breakdown of room types across neighborhood groups.
- **📈 Room Pricing Trends**: How average price varies across boroughs and room types.
- **🔟 Top Profitable Listings**: High-revenue listings identified for insights.
- **📅 Impact of Minimum Nights**: Correlation between stay duration and estimated revenue.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- **Tableau** – For dashboard visualization and analysis
- **Excel / CSV** – Raw data handling
- **Python / Pandas** 

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

- **Manhattan** dominates revenue generation, followed by Brooklyn.
- **Entire homes/apartments** are most popular across all price categories.
- Listings with high minimum nights often underperform in revenue.
